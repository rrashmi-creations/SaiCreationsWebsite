Delivery, Pickup & Open Box

Delivery coverage

Kolkata and nearby areas, plus other Indian pin codes where our courier partner provides service. Availability and charges are confirmed at checkout.

If you are not at home

  1. Delivery partner may call the registered number.
  2. You may request another delivery attempt or authorize an adult recipient.
  3. After permitted attempts, the parcel may be returned to sender; applicable return shipping/COD rules will be disclosed.
  4. Keep your phone reachable and provide correct address and landmark.

Return pickup & drop-off

Once a return is approved, pickup from your address may be arranged if service is available. Otherwise, we may provide an authorized courier drop-off option. Keep the receipt/tracking number.

Open Box Delivery

Available only for eligible pin codes/orders if supported by the courier partner. Follow the courier's inspection instructions. If unavailable, record an unboxing video immediately after delivery and report damage/mismatch promptly.

Delivery attempts, pickup, drop-off, open-box inspection, charges and timelines depend on courier-partner coverage and service terms. Do not hand over a parcel without a valid pickup/return receipt.

Store Policies & Customer Responsibilities

Proposed policy draft for Sai Creation. Final contact details, operational timelines and legal review must be completed before public launch.

1. Return window

  • Regular eligible products: return request within 7 calendar days from delivery.
  • Premium/high-value products: return request within 3 calendar days from delivery; the shorter period must be clearly shown on the product page and before checkout.
  • Wrong, damaged, defective or materially-not-as-described items: contact us promptly; statutory consumer rights and remedies remain unaffected.
  • Return eligibility and available remedy (replacement, refund or other lawful resolution) will be assessed under applicable law.

2. Tags, condition & packaging

  • For change-of-mind returns, original tags, labels, unused/unwashed condition and original packaging are required.
  • Do not remove the product tag until you are satisfied with the item.
  • Tag missing/removed may affect a voluntary change-of-mind return, but will not automatically cancel rights for a defective, wrong or misdescribed product.
  • Keep invoice and order ID for support.

3. Defective / wrong product

  • Share order ID, clear photos/video of the item, tag, packaging and shipping label.
  • Report as soon as reasonably possible after delivery so the issue can be documented.
  • Do not use, wash, repair or alter the item while the complaint is reviewed.
  • We will investigate and provide the remedy required by applicable law and the facts of the case.

4. Refund & exchange

  • Refunds are processed to the original payment method where practicable, or another lawful agreed method.
  • Processing begins after return receipt and inspection, without limiting legal deadlines.
  • Shipping/return charges, if any, will be disclosed before purchase and applied only where lawful.
  • Exchange depends on stock availability; if unavailable, applicable refund remedies remain available.

5. Delivery & failed attempts

  • Customer must provide accurate address, PIN code and active phone number.
  • Customer should remain reachable during the delivery window.
  • If absent, courier may call, make another attempt or allow an authorized recipient, subject to courier rules.
  • Repeated failed attempts may result in return-to-origin. Any fee or COD restriction must be disclosed and lawful.

6. Customer responsibilities

  • Review size chart, fabric, colour, product description, price and delivery estimate before ordering.
  • Check parcel condition and follow available open-box instructions.
  • Do not share OTP with anyone except the authorized delivery person at the time of handover.
  • Report suspected fraud, tampering or mismatch promptly.

7. Store responsibilities

  • Display accurate product descriptions, prices, seller identity and contact details.
  • Use reasonable care in packing and dispatching orders.
  • Provide order updates, complaint support and applicable refund/return remedies.
  • Protect customer data and use it only for legitimate order, delivery and support purposes.

8. Privacy & grievance

Customer information is used for order processing, payment, delivery, support and legal compliance. A final privacy notice, data retention details and grievance officer contact must be published before launch.
