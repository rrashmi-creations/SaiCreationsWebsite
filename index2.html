Store Policies & Customer Information

Please read these policies before placing an order. These are draft store terms and require final legal/business review before the store accepts live orders.

Booking & Order Confirmation

  • Orders are requests until confirmed by the store and stock availability is checked.
  • Customer must provide accurate contact and complete delivery address.
  • Prices, applicable taxes and shipping costs should be shown before checkout.
  • Store may contact customer to verify unusual, incomplete or high-risk orders.
  • Cancellation requests are accepted before dispatch where feasible; after dispatch, cancellation may not be possible.

Returns & Exchanges

  • Standard products: request within 7 days of delivery for eligible preference-based returns.
  • Premium/high-value products: request within 3 days where clearly marked on the product page.
  • Keep tags, packaging and item unused/unwashed for preference-based returns.
  • Wrong, defective or materially misdescribed items: contact support promptly; statutory rights and remedies are not restricted by tag conditions.
  • Return pickup/drop-off depends on courier coverage; otherwise customer may be given an authorized drop-off option.

Refunds

  • Refunds are processed after return verification, using the original payment method where practical and legally required.
  • Refund timelines depend on payment provider/bank processing.
  • Shipping charges, non-refundable charges and deductions must be disclosed lawfully before purchase.
  • Defective/wrong item cases will be reviewed under applicable consumer rules.

Shipping & Delivery

  • Delivery dates are estimates and may be affected by carrier delays, weather, customs or incorrect addresses.
  • Customers should provide a reachable phone number and complete address.
  • Carrier may attempt delivery and contact customer for rescheduling, subject to carrier policy.
  • Where customer is unavailable, carrier may retry, hold at a collection point or return parcel, depending on service.
  • Any applicable redelivery, return-to-origin or shipping fees must be disclosed where lawful.

Pickup & Drop-off

  • Store pickup is available only if confirmed by Sai Creation and a pickup location/time is provided.
  • Return pickup is subject to courier service availability.
  • If home pickup is unavailable, customer may use an authorized courier drop-off location with instructions provided by support.
  • Keep the drop-off receipt/tracking number until the return is resolved.

Open-box & Delivery Evidence

  • Open-box inspection is available only where the carrier supports it and the order is marked eligible.
  • Customer may inspect the parcel externally and follow the carrier's inspection rules.
  • Delivery confirmation may use OTP, carrier scan, or proof-of-delivery photo where permitted.
  • Store packing photos and carrier evidence may be retained only for support, dispute and lawful business purposes.
  • Customer privacy must be respected; avoid unnecessary images of faces, interiors or personal documents.

Cash on Delivery (COD)

  • COD is available only for selected products, postal codes and orders.
  • Order confirmation may be required before dispatch.
  • Customer should be reachable and ready to receive the parcel.
  • Repeated refusals or failed deliveries may result in COD restrictions on future orders, subject to fair notice.

International Orders

  • International delivery is offered only to destinations enabled by the store and carrier.
  • International shipping, duties, taxes, customs clearance and import restrictions vary by country.
  • Customer is responsible for disclosed destination-country import charges unless checkout states otherwise.
  • Customs delays, refused parcels, return shipping and refund deductions must be handled according to law and disclosed terms.
  • International returns may require customer to contact support before sending goods.
  • Payments and currency conversion depend on supported gateway, country and bank.

Delivery Partner Terms

  • Partners must handle parcels carefully, use accurate tracking and follow agreed service procedures.
  • Pickup and delivery scans/photos are required only where agreed, supported and lawful.
  • Lost, damaged, delayed, misrouted or tampered parcels must be promptly reported.
  • Customer contact information is used only to perform delivery and support obligations.
  • Partner liability, claims, COD remittance, service levels and insurance are governed by the actual carrier contract; this draft does not replace it.

Product Details & Offers

  • Screen lighting can cause minor colour differences from actual items.
  • Check size, fabric, wash-care and product-specific return eligibility before booking.
  • Offers are time-limited only when an end date is displayed; stock is subject to availability.
  • Wholesale prices/minimum quantities apply only when shown or confirmed by the store.

Privacy & Complaints

  • Personal data is used for order processing, customer support, payment, delivery, returns and legal obligations.
  • Do not share payment credentials or OTPs with anyone.
  • Customer can contact store support to raise a complaint or request data-related help.
  • Grievance contact details must be added before launch.
